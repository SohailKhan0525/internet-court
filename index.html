We’ll keep the fine print short.

Is this an actual court?+

Only in the court of public opinion. Internet Court is for everyday disagreements and a little perspective. A vote here has no legal effect.

Are these real cases and real votes?+

The three cases on this landing page are fictional examples. Demo votes are saved only in your browser. There are no fabricated community counts, live verdicts, or real people behind these examples.

What happens when I file a case?+

You can write a case and save a private draft on this device, then download it as a text file. Public submissions, accounts, and community voting are not enabled in this preview.

What belongs in Internet Court?+

The last slice. A streaming betrayal. Who gets the armrest. Keep it light, leave out names and personal details, and avoid anything that could put a real person at risk.

Does it cost anything to try?+

No. You can explore all three sample cases and draft your own for free. This preview has no subscriptions, payment collection, or paid memberships.

Your draft stays yours

This preview stores demo votes and any draft you choose to save in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to a database or published to other visitors.

You can delete a saved draft in the case form. Clear site data in your browser to remove all local demo votes and drafts. Downloads create a text file on your device.
